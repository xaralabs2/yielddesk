# YieldDesk Product Constitution

_Status: approved and binding for product, engineering, AI, content, and commercial work._

## Purpose

YieldDesk is a cross-border investment-information, education, comparison, and simulation platform covering Nigeria, the United States, and the United Kingdom.

Its operating journey is:

**Discover → Understand → Compare → Simulate → Watch → Monitor**

Registered individuals may save comparisons, assumptions, watchlists, virtual portfolios, and learning history.

## Permanent operating boundary

YieldDesk may:

- publish sourced market and investment information;
- explain financial instruments and general market concepts;
- compare objective characteristics selected by the user;
- calculate deterministic results from visible user-selected assumptions;
- run clearly labelled hypothetical scenarios and virtual portfolios;
- monitor user-selected instruments and factual market changes;
- publish clearly separated advertising from appropriately screened financial firms.

YieldDesk does not:

- recommend an investment, allocation, broker, adviser, or provider;
- advise a user to buy, sell, hold, avoid, or enter at a particular price;
- determine suitability or make a personal recommendation;
- rank paid advertisers as best, trusted, preferred, or recommended;
- open accounts, transmit orders, arrange transactions, execute trades, or negotiate for users;
- hold customer money or securities;
- receive compensation calculated from deposits, assets, purchases, sales, or trade volume.

## Product language

Use: **explore, understand, compare, simulate, watch, monitor, matches your filters, user-selected assumptions, hypothetical result**.

Do not use as YieldDesk conclusions: **buy, sell, hold, accumulate, avoid, actionable, investable, suitable for you, recommended for you, best investment, preferred entry, target allocation, ready to invest**.

References to buying or selling are permitted only when explaining a general concept, quoting clearly attributed third-party source material, or recording a user's already-completed external transaction. They must never be YieldDesk instructions.

## Deterministic comparison standard

Every material comparison result must preserve:

- instrument and native currency;
- source and observation timestamp;
- delayed/live/end-of-day status;
- calculation method;
- user-entered assumptions;
- fees and taxes included or excluded;
- FX and inflation assumptions;
- historical, current, hypothetical, and forecast values as distinct types;
- missing or stale information;
- a plain-language limitation statement.

YieldDesk presents differences and scenario outcomes. The user decides what those differences mean for them.

## AI authority

AI may explain, summarize, compare sourced facts, describe general risks, identify missing information, and narrate deterministic calculations.

AI must not select an investment or provider for a user, prescribe an allocation, issue trade instructions, or allow commercial relationships to influence an answer without prominent disclosure.

Policy enforcement must exist in deterministic application controls and tests, not only in prompts or disclaimers.

## Advertising

Advertising must be clearly labelled and visually separate from organic content. Payment must not alter organic comparisons, search ordering, calculations, watchlists, monitoring, or AI responses.

The initial commercial model uses fixed placement, campaign, sponsorship, or impression-based fees. Transaction-based compensation requires a new legal and product review.

## Initial investment universe

- Nigeria: Treasury bills, FGN bonds, regulated funds, and selected public-market instruments.
- United States: Treasuries, selected ETFs, REITs, and dividend-oriented public securities.
- United Kingdom: gilts, selected ETFs, REITs, and regulated funds.

Expansion requires reliable provenance, permitted data use, and a coherent comparison method.

## Architecture rule

Deterministic engines calculate financial facts. AI explains sourced information and calculations. The human chooses.

Real holdings recorded by a user and virtual simulations remain separate. YieldDesk does not describe either environment as a brokerage account.

## Feature gate

Every new feature must be classified before release as one of:

- **PERMITTED_NOW**
- **REQUIRES_CONTROLS**
- **REQUIRES_COUNSEL_REVIEW**
- **REQUIRES_LICENSED_PARTNER**
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE**

When classification is uncertain, fail closed and do not expose the feature.
