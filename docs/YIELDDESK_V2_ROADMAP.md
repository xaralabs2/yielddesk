# YieldDesk V2 — Core and Expansion Roadmap

_Last updated: 2026-08-26_

## Delivery Strategy

YieldDesk V2 should be built in controlled layers. Trust, reproducible mathematics and investment rigor come before broader automation. The core must prove that YieldDesk can understand, calculate, value, challenge and compare an investment before expansion into portfolio monitoring and learning.

## Phase 0 — Investment Constitution

**Timing:** Weeks 1–2

Build:

- Canonical investment ontology
- INVESTIGATE / INVESTABLE / ACTIONABLE definitions
- THESIS INTACT / WATCH / BROKEN definitions
- Evidence schema
- Fact vs assumption schema
- Asset-class analytical rules
- Benchmark and required-return policy
- Product/regulatory boundary

**Exit condition:** One canonical YieldDesk investment decision model.

## Phase 1 — Intelligence, Mathematics & Trust Foundation

**Timing:** Weeks 3–7

Build:

- Evidence Graph
- Source provenance and freshness
- Assumption Registry
- Deterministic Investment Mathematics Engine
- CAGR, IRR, XIRR, NPV and return calculations
- Nominal NGN, real NGN and USD/home-currency return
- Benchmark framework
- Required Return / Hurdle Rate framework
- Normalized financial/investment data
- Evidence completeness and confidence

**Exit condition:** Every material number and AI claim can be traced to evidence or an explicit assumption, and critical financial calculations are reproducible.

## Phase 2 — Core Investment Brain

**Timing:** Weeks 8–13

Build initially around Nigerian listed equities:

- Research Analyst
- Financial Analyst
- Earnings Normalization
- Cash Conversion
- Business Quality
- Forensic analysis
- Sector-specific valuation
- Bear/Base/Bull scenarios
- Probability-weighted expected value/return
- Market-Implied Expectations
- Expectation Gap
- Margin of Safety
- Expected Return vs Required Return
- Benchmark / opportunity-cost comparison
- Bear / Counter-Thesis Agent
- Investment Committee synthesis

**Hard milestone:** By Week 13, YieldDesk can take a Nigerian listed company from raw evidence to normalized financials, deterministic return/valuation mathematics, market expectations, counter-thesis, margin of safety, required return and an auditable INVESTIGATE / INVESTABLE decision.

## Phase 3 — Capital Allocation & Portfolio Intelligence

**Timing:** Weeks 14–18

Build:

- Opportunity Cost Engine
- Cross-asset comparison
- Illiquidity Premium
- Portfolio Fit Engine
- Factor Exposure Map
- Hidden Concentration Detector
- Correlation analysis
- TWRR / MWRR portfolio performance
- Maximum drawdown and downside measures
- Scenario / stress engine
- Position Sizing
- Buy Zone / Capital Deployment Queue

**Exit condition:** YieldDesk can answer not only “Is this good?” but “Is this the best use of my next ₦X, is the premium sufficient for the risk/illiquidity, and how much should I allocate?”

## Phase 4 — Monitoring, Attribution, Memory & Learning

**Timing:** Weeks 19–24

Build:

- Thesis Ledger
- Thesis Monitor
- Assumption tracker
- Catalyst tracker
- Intelligent alerts
- Corporate action intelligence
- Decision Journal
- Management Promise Tracker
- Return Attribution
- Contribution to Return
- Investor Learning Engine

**Exit condition:** YieldDesk remembers the original thesis, knows what changed, explains why money was made or lost, and learns from outcomes over time.

## Expansion Beyond Week 24

- Real Estate / SPV Intelligence Engine including CAGR, IRR, XIRR, MOIC, cash-on-cash and payback
- Fixed-Income Intelligence Engine including YTM, real yield, duration, modified duration, convexity and credit spread
- Money-Market Intelligence Engine including effective annual yield, fees, liquidity and real return
- Alternatives framework
- Advanced risk analytics including VaR / Expected Shortfall where appropriate
- Dynamic AI screens by asset context
- Advanced YieldIntel natural-language workflows
- Custom reports and playbooks
- API / partner integrations
- Broker/custodian integration only where approved and appropriate
- Mobile application and notification workflows

## Parallel Governance Track

Runs through all phases:

- SEC/regulatory strategy
- AI governance and model oversight
- Calculation versioning and reproducibility
- Audit trails and explainability
- Investor suitability and disclosures where applicable
- Data security and privacy
- Human-in-the-loop controls
- AI evaluation and observability

## Core Product Principle

> AI-dense underneath. Simple above. Evidence first. Deterministic investment mathematics. Expected return must beat the required return and the best relevant alternative. Human-controlled decisions.
