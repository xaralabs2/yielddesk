# YieldDesk V2 — Core and Expansion Roadmap

_Last updated: 2026-08-26_

## Delivery Strategy

YieldDesk V2 should be built in controlled layers. The sequence is designed to prove trust and investment rigor before adding portfolio automation, monitoring, and broader asset-class expansion.

## Phase 0 — Investment Constitution

**Timing:** Weeks 1–2

Build:

- Canonical investment ontology
- INVESTIGATE / INVESTABLE / ACTIONABLE definitions
- THESIS INTACT / WATCH / BROKEN definitions
- Evidence schema
- Fact vs assumption schema
- Asset-class analytical rules
- Product/regulatory boundary

**Exit condition:** One canonical YieldDesk investment decision model.

## Phase 1 — Intelligence & Trust Foundation

**Timing:** Weeks 3–7

Build:

- Evidence Graph
- Source provenance and freshness
- Assumption Registry
- Deterministic Calculation Engine
- Normalized financial/investment data
- Evidence completeness and confidence

**Exit condition:** Every material number and AI claim can be traced to evidence or an explicit assumption.

## Phase 2 — Core Investment Brain

**Timing:** Weeks 8–13

Build initially around Nigerian listed equities:

- Research Analyst
- Financial Analyst
- Earnings Normalization
- Cash Conversion
- Business Quality
- Forensic analysis
- Sector-specific valuation
- Bear/Base/Bull scenarios
- Market-Implied Expectations
- Expectation Gap
- Margin of Safety
- Bear / Counter-Thesis Agent
- Investment Committee synthesis

**Hard milestone:** By Week 13, YieldDesk can take a Nigerian listed company from raw evidence to normalized financials, valuation, counter-thesis, market expectations, margin of safety and an auditable INVESTIGATE / INVESTABLE decision.

## Phase 3 — Capital Allocation & Portfolio Intelligence

**Timing:** Weeks 14–18

Build:

- Opportunity Cost Engine
- Cross-asset comparison
- Portfolio Fit Engine
- Factor Exposure Map
- Hidden Concentration Detector
- Correlation analysis
- Scenario / stress engine
- Position Sizing
- Buy Zone / Capital Deployment Queue

**Exit condition:** YieldDesk can answer not only “Is this good?” but “Is this the best use of my next ₦X, and how much should I allocate?”

## Phase 4 — Monitoring, Memory & Learning

**Timing:** Weeks 19–24

Build:

- Thesis Ledger
- Thesis Monitor
- Assumption tracker
- Catalyst tracker
- Intelligent alerts
- Corporate action intelligence
- Decision Journal
- Management Promise Tracker
- Investor Learning Engine
- Performance attribution

**Exit condition:** YieldDesk remembers the original thesis, knows what changed, and learns from outcomes over time.

## Expansion Beyond Week 24

- Real Estate / SPV Intelligence Engine
- Fixed-Income Intelligence Engine
- Money-Market Intelligence Engine
- Alternatives framework
- Dynamic AI screens by asset context
- Advanced YieldIntel natural-language workflows
- Custom reports and playbooks
- API / partner integrations
- Broker/custodian integration only where approved and appropriate
- Mobile application and notification workflows

## Parallel Governance Track

Runs through all phases:

- SEC/regulatory strategy
- AI governance and model oversight
- Audit trails and explainability
- Investor suitability and disclosures where applicable
- Data security and privacy
- Human-in-the-loop controls
- AI evaluation and observability

## Build Priority if Resources Are Constrained

1. Evidence + provenance
2. Deterministic calculation engine
3. Asset ontology
4. Earnings/cash normalization
5. Sector-specific valuation
6. Bear/Base/Bull
7. Counter-thesis
8. Market-Implied Expectations
9. INVESTIGATE / INVESTABLE / ACTIONABLE
10. Portfolio Fit
11. Thesis monitoring
12. Investor memory

## Core Product Principle

> AI-dense underneath. Simple above. Evidence first. Deterministic financial math. Human-controlled decisions.
